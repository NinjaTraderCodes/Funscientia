### Funscientia — Kingdoms of Discovery
A story-driven science game where players journey through cities and kingdoms, solve challenges, and learn so naturally they forget they are learning.

## About
As a 9 years experienced science teacher, I can tell that today's students are surrounded by fast and interactive content, so they can quickly disengage from long, passive explanations, and find science hard to understand.

Funscientia is a story-driven science adventure game within Douniaverse, designed to transform scientific concepts into immersive exploration, stories and interactive challenges. Players progress through an expanding world, discovering science naturally through play rather than traditional lessons.

The project already has a defined vision, an original visual identity, characters, an educational framework, storyboards and early creative assets. The next step is to transform this foundation into a coded playable prototype.

During the first month, I will develop and test one focused playable experience combining narrative, exploration, scientific problem-solving and progress feedback. This prototype will become the foundation for expanding the game over time.
