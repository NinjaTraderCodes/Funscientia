# Funscientia: your privacy

Funscientia saves your game so you can continue it later, on this device or on another one.

**What we save:** the character you chose, the first name you typed (if you typed one), your language,
and your progress in the game: the places you reached, your discoveries, your answers to the Guardians'
questions and your stars.

**What we never save:** no email, no password, no account, no location, no photo.
Tip: use a nickname, not your full name.

**Where and why:** on your device, and online (with Supabase, a database service) only so that your
save code can bring your game back on another device.

**Who can see it:** only someone who has your save code. The Funscientia team looks only at totals for
the whole game (for example, how many players finished it), never at who you are.

**How long:** a save that nobody has used for 12 months is deleted.
